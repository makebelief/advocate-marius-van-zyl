Advocate Marius van Zyl.

Based in Winterhoek Park, Kariega (Uitenhage), Advocate Marius van Zyl assists with litigation and dispute resolution across civil, criminal, family, labour and commercial law.

His professional profile records an LLB (cum laude), BA (Honours), Diploma in Police Administration and admission as an advocate on 12 January 2006.
