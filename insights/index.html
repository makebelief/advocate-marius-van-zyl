PRACTICAL GUIDE

Understanding the Bail Process

Seek advice promptly

If someone is arrested, speak with a suitably instructed legal practitioner as soon as possible. Urgency and applicable court times affect the procedure.

Prepare the essentials

Record the accused person’s full name, arrest location, police station, case or docket number and any available court date.

Understand that outcomes vary

Bail decisions depend on statutory requirements, the offence, individual circumstances and the court’s assessment. No result can be guaranteed.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

Preparing for Family Court

Organise documents

Useful records may include court orders, relevant communications, financial records and documents relating to children or maintenance.

Define the issue

Consider whether the immediate need relates to contact, divorce, maintenance, an urgent order or compliance with an existing order.

Put children’s interests first

Where children are affected, their best interests are a central legal consideration. Confidential advice can clarify the next steps.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

Before Starting Civil Litigation

Consider timing and limitation periods

Legal claims may be subject to important deadlines. Ask about them early.

Preserve the evidence

Keep agreements, correspondence, invoices, photographs and an accurate chronology of events.

Discuss alternatives and costs

Ask about negotiation, settlement, arbitration where applicable, costs exposure and the strengths and limits of your position.
