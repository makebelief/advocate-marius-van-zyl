WHO WE ARE

Serving people and businesses in the Eastern Cape.

Advocate Marius van Zyl operates from chambers in Kariega (formerly Uitenhage), providing advocacy and legal representation relating to commercial, civil, family, criminal and employment disputes.

His published professional biography confirms admission in January 2006, qualifications in law and police administration, and membership of the National Bar Council of South Africa.

Whether a matter requires urgent court attention or measured preparation, the aim is an informed discussion of options, procedure and the next sensible step.
